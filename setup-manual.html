Set up Bored Room by hand

For an office admin with a few rooms and no IT department. You'll add the room to a Google Calendar, sign in on the iPad with that same Google account, and hang it up. About 15 minutes for the first room, 5 for each one after.

Print this page with ⌘P (Mac) or Ctrl+P (Windows). Links print with their addresses.

Before you start

Has the wall been figured out? Each iPad needs a mount and constant power next to the door, which may mean an outlet or an Ethernet cable run by an electrician. Sort that out first with Mounting and power and its room planning checklist (PDF); it's the slow part, and the part with a real cost.

You need three things: the room set up in Google, a Google account that has the room on its calendar, and an iPad that's ready. Use a Mac or PC for sections A and B.

A. The room exists in Google

If people already add the room to meetings in Google Calendar, skip to B. If not, someone with access to your Google admin console adds it once:

  1. Go to admin.google.com › left menu Directory › Buildings and resources › Manage resources.
  2. If there's no building yet, add one first (Add building; the address can be approximate).
  3. Add a resource (+) and fill in:
    • Category: Meeting space
    • Type: Conference room
    • Building: where the room is
    • Resource name: what the room should be called, both in Google Calendar and on the iPad
    Save.
  4. Repeat for each room. They now show up when anyone creates a meeting.

B. Put the room on the calendar of the account the iPad will use

The iPad signs in with a Google account and shows whichever room you pick from that account's calendars. Pick the account first:

  • Best: a shared office account, like frontdesk@ or office@, if you already have one. It doesn't leave when a person does.
  • Fine: your own work account. Walk-in bookings from the iPad will also show up on your calendar (see Good to know). If you ever leave, whoever replaces you signs the iPad in with their account (two minutes).

Then, signed in as that account:

  1. Go to calendar.google.com.
  2. On the left, next to Other calendars, click + › Browse resources.
  3. Open your building and tick the room. It now appears under Other calendars.
  4. Repeat for each room that will get an iPad.

C. An iPad that's ready

  1. Any iPad on iPadOS 17 or newer. Settings › General › Software Update if it's behind.
  2. Connected to Wi-Fi, and plugged into power where it will hang. The app keeps the screen on, which needs power.
  3. Bored Room installed from the App Store (99¢). Installing needs an Apple ID on the iPad; any Apple ID works, and you can sign out of it afterward (Settings › your name › Sign Out) without losing the app.
  4. Settings › Accessibility › Display & Text Size › Auto-Brightness off. Otherwise the light sensor undoes the after-hours dimming.
  5. Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock › Never.

Checklist

  • The room exists in Google (A)
  • The room is ticked under Other calendars for the account the iPad will use, and you know that account's password (B)
  • iPad on iPadOS 17+, on Wi-Fi, on power, app installed, Auto-Brightness off (C)

Set up the iPad

  1. Open Bored Room and tap Sign in with Google, then the Sign in with Google button.
  2. Sign in with the account from section B.
  3. Google may show a red warning, "Google hasn't verified this app." That's expected while Google reviews Bored Room. Tap Advanced (bottom left), not the blue Back to safety button, then tap the link that appears below it to continue to Bored Room.
  4. On Sign in to Bored Room, check the account shown is the right one and tap Continue.
  5. On Bored Room wants access to your Google Account, a yellow box repeats that the app isn't verified yet. Under Select what Bored Room can access, tick Select all, scroll down, and tap Continue. One box lets the iPad see your list of calendars (to find the room), the other lets it read the room's meetings and book it from the door. If one is left unticked, Bored Room asks you to sign in again.
  6. Your rooms appear. Tap the one this iPad is for, then Continue.
  7. The room name is filled in. Change it if the board should say something different (Google sometimes adds the capacity, like "(4)"). Continue.
  8. Choose a 4-digit PIN and enter it twice. It protects the settings. Write it down somewhere safe, and use the same PIN on every iPad.
  9. Pick when the board should be on: tap the days, drag the two handles to set the hours (Monday to Friday, 7 AM to 5 PM to start), or switch on Always on. Tap Finish.

The board appears, showing whether the room is free and what's on today. Outside the hours you chose it goes black; touch it and it shows the board for a minute, and a meeting that runs late keeps it on.

Mount it and lock it

  1. Recommended: lock the iPad to the app so nobody wanders off to Safari. Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access › turn it on and set a passcode. Then open Bored Room and press the top button three times quickly. That's the button you normally press to lock the screen or put the iPad to sleep, on the top edge (on many iPads it also has Touch ID built in). Tap Start. To end Guided Access later: press the top button three times again, enter the passcode, tap End. Older iPads with a round Home button below the screen use the Home button instead: press it three times.
  2. Any wall mount or stand that holds an iPad works. Landscape or portrait; the board adapts. Route the power cable. Mounting and power has mount picks, placement, and a clean way to run power.

Changing things later

On the board, press and hold the faint rounded tab in the bottom-right corner for one second. Enter the PIN. Settings opens: room name, which room, show or hide meeting titles, font, background (including your own photo), booking on or off, hours, change PIN. Tap Done to go back to the board.

Forgot the PIN? Tap Forgot PIN? on the PIN screen, sign in again with the same Google account, and pick a new PIN. The iPad stays on the wall.

Good to know

  • The iPad depends on that Google account. If the account is removed, or Google signs it out after a password change, the board says it can't update. Hold the corner tab › PIN › Sign out of Google, then sign in with another account that has the room on its calendar (section B).
  • Bookings from the door show up on the signed-in account's calendar too. When someone taps Book now, the iPad creates the meeting on that account's calendar and invites the room, the same way a meeting booked from a laptop works. So it appears in two places: the room's calendar and the calendar of whoever signed the iPad in, titled "Booked at the door", starting right away, 15 minutes to 2 hours long. It arrives silently: no email, no invite, no reminder. If you'd rather not see these on your own calendar, sign the iPad in with a shared account like frontdesk@, or turn booking off (Settings › Allow booking from this iPad).
  • Private meetings stay private. A meeting marked private shows as "Private meeting" on the board, whoever is signed in.
  • The sign-in never leaves the iPad. Bored Room has no server; the sign-in is kept in the iPad's keychain and used only to talk to Google. See the privacy policy.

If something's off

  • "No rooms found for this account." Section B wasn't done for this account, or you signed in with a different one. Tick the room under Other calendars, then tap Try again.
  • Google says access is blocked, or that your admin hasn't allowed the app. Your organization only lets approved apps use Google accounts. A Google admin allows it once: admin.google.com › Security › Access and data control › API controls › Manage third-party app access › find Bored Room › set it to Trusted. The exact permissions it asks for, and its client ID, are on the privacy page. Or email us and we'll walk them through it.
  • "Book now" isn't on the board. It only appears when the room is free for at least 15 minutes, so it hides just before the next meeting and while the room is busy. If it never appears, booking is turned off in Settings (Allow booking from this iPad).
  • "Book now" doesn't work. Hold the corner tab › PIN › Sign out of Google, sign in again, and tick Select all on Google's permission screen.
  • Meetings show as "Meeting" with no title. "Show meeting titles" is off in Settings.
  • The screen is black. It's outside the hours you set. Touch it to see the board for a minute; hold the corner tab › PIN › Settings › Hours to change the schedule.
  • You forgot the PIN. Hold the corner tab › Forgot PIN? › sign in again with the same Google account › new PIN.
