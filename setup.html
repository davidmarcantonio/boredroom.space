Set up Bored Room

Pick the guide that matches how you manage iPads. Each one stands on its own and prints cleanly, so you can hand it to whoever's doing the work.

By hand

For an office admin, no IT background needed

One to five rooms. You'll click through Google's admin pages once, then set up each iPad in about two minutes. Every step spelled out.

About 20 minutes the first time

With Apple Configurator

For the one-person IT shop

Supervise the iPads from a Mac, install the app through Apple Business Manager, and lock each one to the board so it survives reboots.

About 30 minutes, then 5 per iPad

Through your MDM

For IT teams with Jamf, Mosyle, Kandji, Intune, or similar

Push the app, the kiosk lock, and every setting including the room and the credential. iPads go from the box to the board untouched.

About 30 minutes, then zero per iPad

All three use the same Google setup. A free service account that your admin shares each room with. It's covered in full in the by-hand guide and summarized in the other two. If you'd rather do it on a call, book a free setup session.
