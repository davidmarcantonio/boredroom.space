Set up Bored Room

Pick the guide that matches how you manage iPads. Each one starts with a "Before you start" section and a checklist (accounts, access, downloads), then the hands-on part. They print cleanly, so you can hand the prerequisites to the Google admin and the rest to whoever's holding the iPad.

By hand

For an office admin, no IT background needed

A few rooms. Add the room to your Google Calendar, sign in on the iPad with the same account, pick the room, hang it up. No Google Cloud, no admin settings beyond creating the room.

About 15 minutes the first time, 5 per iPad after

With Apple Configurator

For the one-person IT shop

Supervise the iPads from a Mac, install the app through Apple Business Manager, and lock each one to the board so it survives reboots.

About 30 minutes, then 5 per iPad

Through your MDM

For IT teams with Jamf, Mosyle, Iru (Kandji), Intune, or similar

Push the app, the kiosk lock, and every setting including the room and the credential. iPads go from the box to the board untouched.

About 30 minutes, then zero per iPad

Two ways to connect to Google. By hand, the iPad signs in with a Google account that has the room on its calendar. The Configurator and MDM guides use a free Google service account instead, so no one's account lives on the iPad. If you'd rather do it on a call, book a free setup session.
